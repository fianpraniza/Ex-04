# M8 - Advanced Apple AI

## Objective

Explore advanced Apple AI techniques only after the core deployment path is established.

## Why This Milestone Exists

This elective deepens specialization without delaying the primary on-device production pipeline.

## Competencies

L1 explain the chosen advanced area; L2 build a bounded prototype; L3 debug profiler/tooling findings; L4 compare advanced alternatives; L5 decide whether complexity is justified.

## Concepts

Core AI, Apple Silicon workflows, PyTorch Apple ecosystem, optimization tooling, debugging, profiling, Metal, custom kernels.

## Required Projects

Complete one bounded advanced Apple AI investigation relevant to the capstone.

## Required Experiments

Benchmark an advanced optimization only if its hypothesis and cost justify it.

## Required Evidence

Prototype, profiler findings, benchmark, decision record, and reflection.

## Expected Outputs

An elective portfolio artifact with a clear adoption or rejection decision.

## Dependencies

M7 end-to-end performance baseline. This milestone is elective.

## Definition of Done

One advanced option is evaluated with evidence, trade-offs, and a documented decision; it does not block capstone completion.
